Our Services

There is no all-or-nothing package. Choose only the services most valuable to you.

Financial Administration

Bills paid, books balanced, cash flow clear.

  • Bill payment, budgeting, and cash flow management.
  • Bookkeeping and consolidated financial reporting.
  • Bank account oversight, reconciliation, and coordination of large purchases and money transfers.
  • Real estate accounting, including separate books for each property and holding entity, rental income and expense tracking, and property-level reporting.

Tax & Compliance

Tax planning all year, not just at filing time.

  • Proactive, year-round tax planning in coordination with your tax preparer.
  • Facilitation of annual tax return preparation through coordinated delivery of complete and organized records.
  • Charitable contribution tracking and documentation.
  • Centralized collection, organization, and tracking of K-1s, 1099s, and supporting tax documentation.

Risk Management & Insurance

Coverage reviewed and kept current as your life changes.

  • Regular insurance reviews alongside independent insurance professionals.
  • Ongoing coordination of insurance coverages for newly acquired and disposed-of assets.
  • Assistance with claims coordination and communication.
  • Streamlined communication with insurance carriers on policy administration and renewals.
  • Centralized organization and storage of insurance documents.

Investment Coordination

Every account and manager in one clear report.

  • Consolidated reporting compiled from information provided by custodians and external investment managers.
  • Tracking and administration of capital calls, distributions, liquidity events, and related cash flow obligations.
  • Coordination of administrative matters with external investment advisers, including reporting, documents, deadlines, and meetings.

Estate & Legal

Your plans organized and followed through with your counsel.

  • Coordination of estate planning matters alongside external counsel.
  • Centralized organization and management of legal and estate-related documents.
  • Periodic review of beneficiary designations in coordination with external counsel.
  • Oversight of general wealth transfer logistics.
  • Facilitation of entity formation, restructuring, and business transition planning discussions alongside external advisors.

Document Management

Every important record secure and easy to find.

  • Maintenance of a secure, centralized digital document repository.
  • Organization of financial, legal, and personal records.
  • Password management support (as requested).

Family Governance & Philanthropy

Shared values and giving, organized across generations.

  • Coordination of philanthropic planning efforts alongside external legal, tax, and charitable advisors.
  • Administrative support for charitable vehicles (e.g., foundations, donor-advised funds).
  • Facilitation of family wealth transition discussions and intergenerational communication.
  • Facilitation of family meetings and long-term planning discussions to support alignment across generations.
  • Guidance in the development of family mission, vision, and values statements, as well as a family governance framework.

Next Generation Education

Practical money skills for the next generation.

  • One-on-one or group financial education.
  • Foundational education in investing, budgeting, and financial literacy.
  • Customized education based on age, experience, and family goals.

Travel & Property

Homes and travel coordinated so you don’t have to.

  • Coordination of family travel alongside trusted travel agents.
  • Coordination of property management needs with local property management companies.
