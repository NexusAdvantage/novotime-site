Why I started NovoTime

For years, a dear friend told me his advisors were doing right by him. Then one day he came to me and said, “I have lost a lot of money, I no longer work, and everyone is shrugging their shoulders and blaming it on lack of information.” When I looked into his eyes, I saw that he had lost more than money. He had lost trust and peace of mind.

Each of his advisors had done their own job well, but no one saw the full picture. He didn’t know which questions to ask, what information to share, or, most importantly, how to put the advice into action. Nobody was connecting the dots.

I brought the right professionals together. Today, he’s thriving. But I couldn’t unsee the problem.

I knew there was a better way because I had lived it. After five years in traditional wealth management, I spent most of my career inside a single-family office, first as Chief Investment Officer and later also as Chief Operating Officer. There, I experienced the difference an independent team can make: people who see the whole picture, answer only to the family, and have nothing to sell.

Too often, that kind of support is reserved for those wealthy and experienced enough to build it themselves. I don’t believe it should be.

I started NovoTime so that people who work hard for what they have can have that same coordination, an advocate in their corner who takes on the messy work, and the confidence that someone is looking ahead and following through on their behalf.

I don’t measure a client by the zeroes. I ask one question: Can my team make a real, consistent, and lasting difference in your life?

Bring me the problems everyone else doesn’t want to deal with. That’s where we do our best work.

I started NovoTime to help you reclaim what money alone can’t buy: your time and peace of mind.

Diana NovoselskaFounder
