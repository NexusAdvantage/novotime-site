Why I started NovoTime.

For years, a dear friend told me his advisers were doing right by him. Then one day he came to me and said, “I have lost a lot of money, I no longer actively work, and everyone is shrugging their shoulders and blaming it on lack of information.” It was clear he had lost more than money. He had lost trust and peace of mind.

Each of his advisers had done their own job well, but no one saw the full picture. He didn’t know which questions to ask, what information to share, or, most importantly, how to put the advice into action. Nobody was connecting the dots.

Once we brought the right professionals together, he got back on solid ground. But I couldn’t unsee the problem.

I knew there was a better way because I had lived it. After five years in traditional wealth management, I spent most of my career inside a single-family office, first as Chief Investment Officer and later also as Chief Operating Officer. Every day, I saw what an independent team could do: see the whole picture, anticipate what was coming, and make sure nothing fell through the cracks. It taught me something I still believe: good advice is only as valuable as what happens next.

Too often, that kind of support is reserved for those wealthy and experienced enough to build it themselves. I don’t believe it should be.

So I set out to build something different. Knowing the investment world from the inside, I deliberately chose not to start another wealth management firm. I wanted our independence to be beyond question. We sell no financial products and earn no commissions, from anyone, on anything.

NovoTime gives people who have worked hard for what they have an advocate in their corner: someone who handles the complexities, brings the right people together, looks ahead, and follows through.

Our name reflects that promise. Novo means new, and while you can always earn more money, you can never buy back time. NovoTime is about giving you new time, and the freedom to devote it to whatever matters most to you. And knowing someone is looking out for your interests is where peace of mind begins.

We don’t measure a client by the zeroes. We ask one question: Can our team make a real, consistent, and lasting difference in your life?

Bring us the problems everyone else doesn’t want to deal with. That’s where we do our best work.

Diana V. NovoselskaFounder
