True wealth is rarely defined by the numbers on a statement.

It is reflected in the time you reclaim, the peace of mind you gain, the legacy you build, and the freedom to direct your attention to what matters most. That is what NovoTime is built to protect.

We serve as the dedicated team behind your financial life, bringing coordination, execution, and ongoing attention to whatever you entrust to us. We are involved in the day-to-day, not just the big decisions.

Our approach.

Discretion

Confidentiality is at the heart of our work and of the trust our clients place in us. Every member of our team carries that commitment into every conversation, document, and decision, and our technology and internal operations are held to the highest standards of privacy and security.

Continuity

The professionals who know our clients’ affairs today will know them next year, and the year after. We believe enduring relationships build deeper understanding and wiser decisions. We work with only as many clients as we can serve well, which lets us stay personally involved and accountable.

Coordination

We strengthen the work of our clients’ trusted advisors by working alongside them, not in place of them. With a full view of their affairs, we connect their efforts, identify gaps early, and bring every part of their financial life together.

Customization

Every engagement is tailored to the client, from how often we communicate and how much detail they prefer, to the types of reports that work best for them. As needs change with life’s major milestones, so does our engagement.

There is nothing more important to us than our independence, which allows us to stand fully on your side.

We do not provide investment advice, portfolio management services, or securities recommendations. This separation from the investment function is deliberate and foundational to our approach. It means we can support you with complete objectivity, free from the conflicts of interest that can arise when product sales and advisory services are combined.

How our engagement works.

Flat monthly fee

We charge a flat monthly fee, agreed upon at the start of each engagement, based on the services selected and their complexity. The monthly commitment stays the same, changing only if the engagement does.

Only what you need

There is no all-or-nothing package; clients choose only the services most valuable to them.

Nothing to sell

We earn no commissions or third-party incentives, and our only product is the work we do every day for the people we serve.

Every dollar watched

We watch every dollar as if it were our own, continually looking for opportunities to reduce unnecessary costs and identify savings for our clients. That same stewardship guides the way we price our work.
